## Law

- **[Abortion pill dispute returns to Supreme Court](https://www.scotusblog.com/2026/05/abortion-pill-dispute-returns-to-supreme-court/)**

The application puts the Court at the center of post-<em>Dobbs</em> abortion access litigation once again, with major implications for the FDA's authority to regulate drug distribution and for the broader landscape of reproductive rights under federal law.

The decision carries structural consequences for congressional representation in majority-minority districts and sets precedent for redistricting litigation across the country.

- **[State and federal courts jockey for power in the Roundup case and other mass public harms](https://www.scotusblog.com/2026/05/state-and-federal-courts-jockey-for-power-in-the-roundup-case-and-other-mass-public-harms/)**

The extension leaves unresolved the central dispute over "backdoor searches" of Americans' communications collected under the foreign-intelligence program, and signals that a durable legislative fix remains politically out of reach.
